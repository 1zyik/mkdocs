## :rocket: What's Inside?

This documentation is structured around the core pillars of modern infrastructure:

*   **:gear: DevOps Practices**
    Deep dives into CI/CD pipelines, Infrastructure as Code (IaC), and automation strategies. Expect plenty of **Terraform**, **Ansible**, and **GitHub Actions**.

*   **:chart_with_upwards_trend: SRE & Observability**
    Exploring how to keep systems reliable and scalable. Notes on **Prometheus**, **Grafana**, SLIs/SLOs, and incident management.

*   **:house_with_garden: Homelab Adventures**
    The playground where theory meets reality. Documenting my self-hosted services, networking setups, and hardware tinkering.
