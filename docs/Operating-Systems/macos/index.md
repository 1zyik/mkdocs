# Everything macOS

macOS blends a polished desktop experience with a Unix foundation underneath. It is the daily driver of choice for many developers and engineers, offering powerful command-line tooling alongside a refined GUI.

In this section, I document tips, configurations, and deep dives into the macOS ecosystem. From built-in utilities to system behaviour, this is where the Apple lives.

## What to Expect

*   **Terminal & Shell**: Built-in tools, shortcuts, and productivity tricks hiding in plain sight.
*   **System Configuration**: Defaults, preferences, and settings worth knowing.
*   **Developer Tooling**: Environment setup, package management, and workflow optimisation.

## The Philosophy

> "It just works — until you want to know why."

macOS rewards those who look beneath the surface. The Unix core means most Linux knowledge transfers directly, and the built-in tooling is often more capable than it first appears.
